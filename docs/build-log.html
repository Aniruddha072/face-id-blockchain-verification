Face ID + Blockchain Verification

Detects a face, finds it across the public web via reverse-image search, confirms the match with an ensemble of three independent face-verification models, and anchors the result on-chain as a record anyone can check themselves, including how confident the match was.

03
Ensemble verify
3 models vote on each candidate, majority required to pass
04
Anchor on-chain
Hash and agreement count written to a Solidity contract
05
Prove it
Read the chain back, confirm nothing was altered

Why ensemble verification

A single model can confidently match the wrong person. Confirmed with a real test: ArcFace alone passed a stranger's photo under its own threshold. Every candidate here is checked against 3 independent models, and needs a majority to count as genuinely verified, not just one opinion.

Known limitations

  • >Search coverage is limited to what Google has indexed, won't find recent or private posts
  • >A tamper-evident hash on-chain proves the record existed at a point in time, not that the matched content itself is authentic
  • >No liveness or deepfake detection on the input photo
  • >Runs on Polygon's public Amoy testnet, not mainnet, no real funds involved
  • >Built only for use with consenting subjects who have a real public presence
